# /qml-open-problems — essence

**Job.** Turn the open questions the team and the field have already written down into testable
claims, so that questions stop living only in notes.

## Judgment calls
- **Is it still open?** A question answered since it was written is closed; say by what.
- **What is the smallest version that could be decided?** A named model family, size, baseline and
  measurement, not the whole question.
- **Which direction does it serve?** Or does it point at a direction the lab does not have yet?

## Success
Each open question leaves as zero to three testable claims in the backlog, or as a note that it is
answered or not decidable with the lab's means.

## Failure it guards against
Copying questions into the backlog unchanged, and quietly dropping the hard ones.

# /qml-variants — essence

**Job.** Make a closed program pay twice: once for what it ruled out, and again for the closely
related ideas that avoid its specific failure, without losing the classical results and
instruments it produced.

## Judgment calls
- **Should variants be generated at all?** Yes after a confirmed NO-GO or a kill on paper; as extra
  ideas after a provisional NO-GO (never replacing its reopening condition); as ways to loosen the
  conditions of a CONDITIONAL-GO; not after GO or BLOCKED.
- **What exactly failed?** One primary mechanism, stated as a precondition.
- **Is this a new idea or the same one?** Same gate and same mechanism is a covered case of the
  exclusion, not a variant.
- **What else is it worth?** A paper, IP (filing before any disclosure), and the salvage.

## Success
Up to ten variants ranked by potential, each with what changed, why it escapes the failure, a quick
check against the first four gates and its cheapest kill test. A backlog, not a brainstorm. "No
viable variant", with the reason, is also success.

## Failure it guards against
Idea spam, re-arguing the verdict, and the quiet loss of the classical assets a program produced.

# /qml-build-on — essence

**Job.** Do what a working researcher does with a good paper: take one result, theirs or ours, and
propose the next small step on top of it that would be worth publishing.

## Judgment calls
- **Is the base worth building on?** A result that survived our own review, or one the field has
  replicated. A chain of steps inherits the weaknesses of its first link.
- **Which kind of step?** Stress-test the result (stronger baseline, larger size, real data, noise),
  extend it (new task, regime or hardware), combine it with a second result, or generalize it. One
  step, not a programme.
- **What would the step show if it worked, and if it failed?** A good step is publishable either
  way, and names the smallest experiment that decides it.

## Success
A handful of steps per base, each a testable sentence with its base, the kind of step, the rung it
aims at and the experiment that decides it, each entered into the backlog for the common gate.

## Failure it guards against
Restating the base paper as an idea; steps so large they are a research agenda; and grading one's
own steps, which belongs to the gate.

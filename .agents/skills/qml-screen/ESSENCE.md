# /qml-screen — essence

**Job.** Kill ideas on paper, in hours, before they cost compute; when an idea survives, say
exactly what shape it survives in.

## Judgment calls
- **Has the lab seen this before?** A prior-work lookup comes first, then the exclusion ledger is
  engaged by mechanism: same mechanism, or a changed precondition?
- **Which rule decides it?** Input loading and readout (G1), whether classical is a real bottleneck
  (G2), the classical competitor checklist (G3), then real data, the operating point and the
  resource range. At least one must be decisive, or the hypothesis is not sharp enough yet.
- **Where could the quantum step sit?** One deployment may survive where the others fail.
- **Is this a question for literature?** Then ask deep research a scoped question and resume; do
  not guess.

## Success
PASS with the constraints the pre-registration must honor; PASS-NARROWED with the smaller claim
that survives; or KILLED-ON-PAPER with a named mechanism and the narrowest class it covers, which
becomes a ledger entry.

## Failure it guards against
"Promising, needs an experiment" on every axis, and its opposite: killing a whole field because one
construction failed.

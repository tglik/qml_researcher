# /qml-verdict — essence

**Job.** Say what the program found against the gates frozen before it started, at exactly the
strength the evidence supports, in words a decision-maker can act on and a future researcher can
reopen from.

## Judgment calls
- **Which category.** GO (with its advantage type), CONDITIONAL-GO (with testable conditions),
  NO-GO-FINAL (all five negative standards hold), NO-GO-PROVISIONAL (one fails, most often an
  inadequate baseline), or BLOCKED.
- **"Not shown in the tested range" or "shown to be absent",** per hypothesis.
- **What the mechanism is,** structurally, with the narrowest class it covers.
- **What survives regardless:** classical results, instruments and datasets that transfer.

## Success
Every number traceable, wording never above the claim ladder, a mechanism that could become a
ledger entry verbatim, and a "survives" section someone will reuse.

## Failure it guards against
Overclaiming a GO, and writing a final NO-GO over a weak baseline or an underpowered test.

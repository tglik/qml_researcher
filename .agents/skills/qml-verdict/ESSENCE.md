# /qml-verdict — essence

## What this skill is for
Saying what the program found, against the gates that were frozen before it started, at exactly
the strength the evidence supports — in words a decision-maker can act on and a future
researcher can reopen from.

## The judgment calls it makes
- **Which category.** GO (with its advantage type), CONDITIONAL-GO (with testable conditions),
  NO-GO-FINAL (all five negative standards hold), NO-GO-PROVISIONAL (one fails — most often a
  baseline that was not adequate), or BLOCKED.
- **"Not shown in the tested range" or "shown to be absent"** — per hypothesis. They are
  different claims about different things.
- **What the mechanism is.** Structural, with the narrowest class it generalizes to.
- **What survives regardless.** The classical results, instruments and datasets that transfer
  even when the quantum claim dies.

## What success looks like
Every number traceable, wording never above the claim ladder, a mechanism that could become a
ledger entry verbatim, and a "survives" section someone will actually reuse.

## The failure it guards against
Overclaiming a GO — and, in a lab that rewards kills, writing a final NO-GO over a weak baseline
or an underpowered test.
